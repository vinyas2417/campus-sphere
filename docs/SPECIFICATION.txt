21. SYNCHRONIZATION AND CONCURRENCY

Synchronization must be demonstrated through a meaningful feature.

Resource Booking

If two users attempt to reserve:

AI Lab

10:00–11:00

at approximately the same time,

only one valid booking may succeed.

Before inserting the booking:

1. Begin transaction.
2. Recheck availability.
3. Detect overlapping reservations.
4. Lock or synchronize critical operation where needed.
5. Insert booking.
6. Commit transaction.

If conflict exists:

Display:

Booking Conflict

“AI Lab is already reserved during the selected time.”

Faculty Reallocation

Two administrators must not allocate the same substitute faculty member to overlapping classes.

Revalidate faculty availability immediately before final assignment.
